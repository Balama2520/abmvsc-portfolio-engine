Step 1 of 6 · You

Launch your website

Everything you filled in is already written into the site. Download it as one ZIP, unzip it, and put the folder online. There is nothing to install and no build step.

  1. Download your website (ZIP)

    The archive holds the pages, the styles, the script, your photo, your resume and a small settings file.

  2. Unzip the folder

    Double-click the ZIP, or right-click it and choose Extract.

  3. Drag the folder onto Netlify Drop

    Open app.netlify.com/drop and drop the folder in. An account is optional.

  4. Copy the address Netlify gives you

    That address is your live site. GitHub Pages and Cloudflare Pages work the same way — the README has the steps for both.

What now?

  1. Unzip the folder.

    Double-click the ZIP, or right-click it and choose Extract. The folder holds your whole site.

  2. Open Netlify Drop.

    app.netlify.com/drop — an account is optional.

  3. Drag the unzipped folder onto the page.

    Netlify uploads it and shows a live address in a few seconds.

  4. Copy that address.

    It is your site. You can rename it under Netlify's site settings.
